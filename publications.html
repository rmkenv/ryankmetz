Books & Guides

AI Policy Primer

A concise introductory guide ideal for newcomers to the complex realm of AI policy. This essential primer explores current AI regulations, ethical considerations, and the interplay between technology and environmental sustainability. Perfect for professionals, policymakers, and students seeking to understand AI's role in building a sustainable future.

Building Decarbonization Pathways

A comprehensive analysis of pathways and solutions for decarbonizing the building sector, which is responsible for nearly 40% of global greenhouse gas emissions. This work explores technological, policy, and behavioral interventions necessary to achieve climate goals through the built environment.

Decarbonizing Educational Institutions

Examination of strategies and barriers to decarbonizing educational institutions in the United States, with focus on systemic change and student engagement. Provides frameworks for academic institutions to transition to carbon neutrality.

Python Tools for Climate Data Analysis

Practical guide to Python tools and libraries for analyzing climate data and environmental justice issues, with code examples and applications. Perfect for data scientists and environmental professionals.

Academic Research

GIS Education & Sustainability Engagement

Analysis of how GIS education and geospatial literacy contributes to sustainability engagement and environmental awareness among students. Demonstrates the educational impact of spatial thinking.

Business Continuity & Crisis Management

Framework for business continuity planning in crisis situations with focus on adaptive management and stakeholder communication. Applies to climate adaptation and resilience planning.
