Climate & Environmental Data

FEMA NFIP Claims Analysis

Provides seamless interaction with the FEMA NFIP Claims API, enabling users to access detailed data on National Flood Insurance Program claims. Users can query flood patterns, claims history, and risk assessments across the United States.

Applications: Flood risk assessment, climate adaptation planning, emergency management

Environmental Justice Data Guide

A comprehensive guide for insightful environmental justice data analysis and interpretation. Helps users understand disparities in environmental burden and benefits across communities.

Applications: Community advocacy, policy research, environmental justice assessment

Federal Funding Guide

Guide on federal funding opportunities and requirements including IRA, BIL, Davis Bacon Act, and others. Helps organizations identify and navigate funding pathways for sustainability projects.

Applications: Sustainability project financing, grants management, organizational planning

Advanced Analytics & Visualization Tools

Interactive Data Platform

Python/Streamlit application designed to help users visualize and interact with geospatial and tabular data. Features interactive mapping, data filtering, and export capabilities.

Use Cases: Environmental monitoring, urban planning, climate analysis

Plant Species Identification

Python web application leveraging Pl@ntNet's API to identify plant species on campus. Supports biodiversity research and environmental education through crowdsourced observations.

Applications: Biodiversity monitoring, environmental education, species tracking

Urban Tree Canopy Analysis

Remote sensing product using 50 cm resolution multispectral imagery, LIDAR, and US Forestry's Climate Change Atlas to map and analyze urban tree canopy cover and change.

Applications: Urban resilience, heat mitigation, forest management
