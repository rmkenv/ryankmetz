About My Craft

Woodworking has always been a deep passion of mine. In my free time, I love building furniture with traditional hand tools and carving spoons from greenwood. This craft combines technical skill, artistic expression, and connection to nature—values that align perfectly with my sustainability mission.

All materials are sustainably sourced from Maryland, Virginia, and West Virginia, supporting local forests and craftspeople. I'm committed to environmentally responsible practices throughout the creation process.

Products

Hand-Carved Spoons

Each spoon is meticulously hand-carved from locally sourced green wood using traditional carving techniques. These functional works of art are perfect for cooking, serving, or display.

Spoon Categories

  • Kitchen Spoons: Perfect for daily kitchen use. Durable, beautiful, and designed for comfort and function. Available in various wood species and finishes.
  • Serving Spoons: Serving spoons, slotted spoons, and specialty designs for specific culinary purposes. Each designed with both aesthetics and functionality in mind.
  • Art Spoons: Larger, intricately carved spoons that serve as decorative art pieces while remaining fully functional.

Wooden Pens

Beautiful, functional pens handcrafted from sustainably sourced wood. Each pen features high-quality hardware and smooth writing performance. Perfect for professionals, collectors, and gift-giving.

Custom Orders

I accept custom orders for specific spoon designs, pen styles, or other wooden items. Each custom piece is created to your specifications using sustainable materials.

Interested in a custom piece? Please contact me with details about your vision, and I'll work with you to create something truly special.

Sustainability Commitment

All wood is sourced from sustainable suppliers in the Mid-Atlantic region. I prioritize:

  • Local Sourcing: Supporting regional forestry and wood suppliers
  • Responsible Harvesting: Working with suppliers committed to sustainable practices
  • Waste Reduction: Minimizing waste through efficient design and use of all materials
  • Traditional Techniques: Using hand tools and time-honored methods
  • Quality Craftsmanship: Building lasting items designed to endure generations

Why Handcrafted Matters

In a world of mass production, handcrafted items represent a commitment to quality, individuality, and sustainability. Each piece carries the mark of skilled craftsmanship and embodies a connection between maker and user. By choosing handcrafted goods, you're investing in durability, artistry, and environmental responsibility.

About Sustainable Wood Sourcing

Sustainable forestry practices ensure that forests can continue to provide resources for future generations. By sourcing wood responsibly and working with local suppliers, I support healthy forests and vibrant communities in the Mid-Atlantic region.

Every handcrafted piece is an investment in a more sustainable future—one spoon at a time.
