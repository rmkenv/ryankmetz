About Me

Professional background, mission, and core values

Background & Experience

With over a decade of vibrant career experience, I've dedicated myself to advancing sustainability, climate resilience, and data-driven decision-making. My background spans environmental science, geospatial analysis, energy analytics, and organizational leadership. I've worked across various sectors including consulting, government, academia, and technology to drive meaningful environmental impact.

My Mission

To facilitate and accelerate the transition to a more sustainable and resilient society. I believe in harnessing technology, policy, and community engagement to create scalable solutions to environmental challenges. Whether through data science, AI applications, or strategic planning, my goal is to empower organizations and communities to take meaningful climate action.

Core Values

Innovation & Continuous Learning

Constantly seeking and evaluating cutting-edge solutions to enhance sustainability and respond to climate change. I'm passionate about exploring new technologies and methodologies that can accelerate environmental progress.

Collaboration & Partnerships

Believing in the power of diverse partnerships and cross-sector cooperation to achieve the greatest impact. Solutions are most powerful when developed collectively, bringing together expertise from multiple disciplines and communities.

Knowledge Sharing & Capacity Building

Committing to share knowledge and empower others to take action. I believe in making complex topics accessible and building capacity across organizations and communities to drive widespread adoption of sustainable practices.

Equity & Environmental Justice

Ensuring that our sustainable future includes all communities, especially the most vulnerable. Environmental justice is central to all sustainability efforts, and solutions must address the disproportionate impacts on frontline communities.

Areas of Expertise

  • Climate Resilience: Assessing vulnerability, planning adaptation strategies, and building infrastructure resilience
  • Decarbonization: Pathways for buildings, energy systems, transportation, and industrial processes
  • Environmental Justice: Data analysis, policy, and community-centered solutions
  • Data Science & Analytics: Machine learning, forecasting, and predictive modeling
  • Geospatial Analysis: Remote sensing, GIS, and earth observation applications
  • Energy Systems: Optimization, forecasting, and demand management
  • Sustainability Strategy: Corporate ESG, reporting, and transformation
  • Open Source Development: Building accessible tools for environmental analysis

Education & Credentials

Continuous professional development in climate science, data science, geospatial technologies, and sustainability. Active participation in industry conferences, workshops, and collaborative research initiatives.

Let's Connect

I'm always interested in connecting with collaborators, organizations, and communities working toward sustainability and climate resilience. Whether you're interested in consulting, collaboration, speaking engagements, or simply connecting over shared interests—I'd love to hear from you.
